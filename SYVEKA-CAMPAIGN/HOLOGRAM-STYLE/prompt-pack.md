# Hologram style — prompt pack for more photoreal clips

The reference clip (`source/reference-clip.mp4`) is photoreal AI footage. Footage like it can only come
from an AI video model. From this coding session every Higgsfield generation costs credits (a 5-second
clip quoted 22.5 credits; the balance is 10) and the unlimited allowance is refused here. Higgsfield
itself says the unlimited allowance works in **Claude chat, Claude Cowork and ChatGPT**. So: paste the
prompts below there, with the unlimited option on, and send me the clips — I will cut them into
finished ads with captions, music and the Syveka end card, at no cost.

## How to make each ad longer than 15 seconds

One generation is about 5 seconds, so each ad is three shots of the same person in the same place:
**1 problem → 2 the assistant appears → 3 relief.** Generate the still for shot 1 first, then use it as
the start image for all three shots so the person and room stay the same. I slow the footage slightly
and add the Syveka scenes and end card, which gives 20–25 seconds.

Settings for every shot: model Seedance 2.0 (or 2.5), 9:16, 5 seconds, 720p or higher, no audio.
Always end the prompt with: `No text, no logos, no readable signage, interface elements abstract and unreadable.`
(The reference clip has garbled letters on its cards; this line avoids that. Real text is added in the edit.)

## Shared look (put this at the start of every prompt)

`Cinematic photorealistic commercial shot, natural daylight, shallow depth of field, soft bokeh, realistic skin and hands, calm smooth camera.`

## Hologram description (use the same words every time)

`a translucent glowing blue-violet holographic human-shaped assistant made of light stands on the surface, soft light rings on the surface beneath it, frosted-glass interface cards with simple icons float in the air around it`

## Six scenarios

### 1. Hair salon
- **Still:** a hairdresser in her 30s in a bright Nordic salon, combing a seated client's hair, a phone lighting up on the marble counter in the foreground.
- **Shot 1:** she keeps cutting with both hands busy while the phone on the counter lights up again and again; she glances at it, slightly stressed. Slow push-in.
- **Shot 2:** [hologram description] appears on the counter beside the phone; glass cards with a calendar icon and a contact icon rise around it. Camera slowly orbits the counter.
- **Shot 3:** the hairdresser looks over, smiles with relief and goes back to her client; the hologram and cards glow softly in the background. Slow pull-back.

### 2. Auto workshop
- **Still:** a mechanic in his 40s in a clean Finnish workshop under a raised car, a phone on a red tool cabinet.
- **Shot 1:** he works with a wrench as the phone lights up and vibrates; he sighs and wipes his hands. Slow push-in, tungsten work lights, light haze.
- **Shot 2:** [hologram description] appears on the tool cabinet; cards with a calendar icon and a wrench icon float beside it. Low-angle orbit.
- **Shot 3:** the mechanic nods, relaxed, and returns to the car; the hologram stays glowing on the cabinet. Slow dolly back.

### 3. Cleaning company
- **Still:** a cleaning company owner in a neat uniform beside a white van in crisp morning light, holding a clipboard and a phone.
- **Shot 1:** she juggles the clipboard and the buzzing phone, overwhelmed. Handheld feel, slow push-in.
- **Shot 2:** [hologram description] appears above the van's open rear floor; cards with a list icon and a calendar icon line up beside it. Gentle orbit.
- **Shot 3:** she lowers the phone, exhales and smiles; the cards settle into a tidy column. Slow pull-back.

### 4. Restaurant
- **Still:** a restaurant owner at a host stand in a warm bistro before opening, a reservation book and a phone in front of him.
- **Shot 1:** he holds the phone to his ear while writing in the book and raises a hand to a waiting guest. Slow dolly-in, warm practical lights.
- **Shot 2:** [hologram description] appears on the host stand; cards with a calendar icon and a table icon float around it. Slow orbit.
- **Shot 3:** he puts the phone down, smiles and greets the guest; the hologram glows beside the book. Slow pull-back.

### 5. Solo consultant at home office
- **Still:** a woman in her 30s at a small home-office desk with a laptop, sticky notes on the wall, evening light.
- **Shot 1:** she rubs her forehead looking at the notes and the laptop. Slow push-in.
- **Shot 2:** [hologram description] appears on the desk beside the laptop; the sticky notes are echoed by neat glass cards with contact and document icons. Slow orbit.
- **Shot 3:** she leans back and smiles, relaxed; the cards hover in an ordered row. Slow pull-back.

### 6. Shop counter
- **Still:** a shop owner in an apron at a light birch counter in a small Finnish shop, a laptop and a phone on the counter, winter daylight.
- **Shot 1:** the phone buzzes and the laptop pings while a customer waits; the owner looks torn between them. Slow handheld push-in.
- **Shot 2:** [hologram description] appears on the counter; cards with a chat icon and a calendar icon rise around it. Slow orbit.
- **Shot 3:** the owner turns to the customer with a calm smile; the hologram and cards glow at the edge of frame. Slow pull-back.

## Honesty notes for these scenes

- The hologram is a metaphor. Each ad that uses such footage carries a "Dramatization" label.
- Do not show the hologram answering a phone call: voice answering is not available in Syveka today.
  Calendar, contact, document and chat icons match what the pilot actually offers.

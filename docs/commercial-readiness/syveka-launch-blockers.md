# Syveka — launch blockers (2026-11-08)

## Severity scale

- **P0**: security, data loss, cross-tenant access.
- **P1**: a core journey can't complete reliably.
- **P2**: important reliability, validation or operational problem.
- **P3**: low impact.

## The two launch tiers

- **Pilot**: a free, controlled pilot on the FREE plan with grants.
- **Paid**: public paid launch.

**Owner** is who must act: Owner means a human with credentials or authority; Claude means
the work can be finished in the repository. **No P0 was found** in the launch-candidate
modules (independent tenant-isolation and RBAC review, 2026-11-08).

**Defects found and fixed in this round by the new tests and reviews:**

1. **Booking widget:** the "slot taken" message was wiped by the slot reload, so the guest's form just disappeared.
2. **Manage page:** rescheduling into a time taken meanwhile showed "reschedule failed" instead of "slot taken".
3. **Booking widget:** a stale error stayed visible in a newly chosen slot's form.
4. **Schedule save:** a failed save could leave a new timezone over the old rules.
5. **KB discard (review, blocking):** a refused double-submit could delete the file of a just-created document.
6. **Chat stream:** a slow Redis release kept the finished SSE stream open.
7. **Members page:** a refused member change looked saved (role), or replaced the page (removal).
8. **Billing:** a canceled row's old paid plan could be the basis for a raise.
9. **Dunning clock:** an old retried event could start the clock early, shortening the grace.

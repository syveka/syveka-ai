# Questions for Fish Audio (draft, not sent)

For the owner to review and send, if at all. **Owner decision of 2026-11-05: do not adopt Fish, do not
replace Vapi, do not purchase credits, and do not run paid evaluations.** These questions commit Syveka
to nothing. Asking them only keeps the option informed.

Before sending: confirm the recipient. The terms and enterprise page name Hanabi AI Inc. (Delaware), and
the open-source licence names 39 AI, Inc. Avoid sharing customer names, volumes or roadmap.

---

**Subject: Data protection questions for an EU business using your speech API**

Hello,

We build business software for customers in the EU, and we're evaluating speech providers. We haven't
made a decision and aren't asking for a commercial proposal yet. Could you answer the following in
writing, and tell us which answers depend on plan or contract?

**Contracting party**

1. Which legal entity contracts with customers and acts as data processor? How are Hanabi AI Inc. and
   39 AI, Inc. related?

**Data processing agreement**

2. Do you offer a GDPR Art. 28 data processing agreement on standard API plans, or only on enterprise
   contracts? Please send the current version.
3. Your models documentation mentions "DPA guarantees" for `s2.1-pro`. What does that mean, and does it
   apply to `s2.1-pro-free`?

**Training**

4. Your terms state that "Usage Data and Content may be used to develop, train, or enhance" models. Does
   that apply to text submitted to the API and audio generated from it?
5. Can a customer opt out by contract, and would that cover all models, past submissions and voice
   references?

**Sub-processors**

6. Please provide your current sub-processor list, with each one's role and location, and how customers
   are notified of changes.

**Data location and EU processing**

7. Your enterprise page says data stays in the United States by default, with inference in the US and
   Tokyo. Which data goes to which location, including logs, backups and support access?
8. Can processing, including inference, be restricted to the EU? On which models and plans, and at what
   latency? Your documentation mentions an `eu` region through SLNG. Who is the processor in that case?
9. For transfers out of the EEA, which mechanism do you rely on (for example, the 2021 Standard Contractual
   Clauses)? Have you made a transfer impact assessment available to customers?

**Retention and deletion**

10. How long do you retain submitted text, generated audio, voice references and logs, on standard plans?
11. Is zero data retention available outside enterprise contracts? What does it exclude?
12. How do you handle deletion requests and data subject requests that reach you through a customer?

**Security**

13. What is the status of SOC 2 Type II, with an expected report date? Do you hold ISO 27001 or similar?
14. How and how quickly do you notify customers of a personal data breach?

**Languages**

15. Which languages does `s2.1-pro` support? Are Finnish and Arabic included, and which Arabic dialects?

**Output and voices**

16. Who owns generated audio, and may customers use it commercially, including on a free model?
17. What consent evidence do you require for cloned voices, and do you retain voice references?

Thank you,

[Name, Syveka]

---

## What an answer would change

The evaluation's data-protection gate stays closed unless Fish provides, in writing, a DPA with a
sub-processor list, EU processing or a defensible transfer mechanism, and a contractual no-training
commitment. Even with all of those, there's still no case to adopt without a listening test, and paid
evaluations are currently not approved. Counsel should read any answer.
